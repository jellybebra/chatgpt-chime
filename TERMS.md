Effective date: October 28, 2026

ChatGPT Chime is a free, independent extension maintained by [jellybebra](https://github.com/jellybebra). It adds a completion sound and an optional blue tab indicator to ChatGPT. It is not an OpenAI product and is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI. ChatGPT and other third-party names and assets belong to their respective owners.

## Using the extension

No separate extension account, payment, or subscription is required. You need a compatible browser and access to ChatGPT under that service's terms. You can disable or uninstall the extension at any time.

The extension detects completion from the page interface. Site changes, suspended or closed tabs, browser restrictions, and unusual response types can prevent or delay notifications. Do not rely on it for time-critical or safety-critical alerts.
